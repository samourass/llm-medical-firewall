# Type 2 Diabetes — General Information

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. General public-health information for this project's RAG
> demo only. Not medical advice, and not derived from any real patient record.

Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition in which the body does not use insulin properly, leading
to elevated blood sugar (glucose) levels over time.

## Common symptoms

- Increased thirst and frequent urination
- Increased hunger
- Fatigue
- Blurred vision
- Slow-healing sores

Many people with type 2 diabetes have mild or no symptoms for years before diagnosis, which is
why routine screening matters for people with risk factors.

## Common risk factors

- Overweight or obesity
- Physical inactivity
- Family history of diabetes
- Age over 45
- History of gestational diabetes

## General lifestyle guidance commonly discussed with patients

- Balanced diet with attention to carbohydrate intake
- Regular physical activity
- Routine blood glucose monitoring, as recommended by a clinician
- Regular follow-up for A1C testing and screening for complications (eyes, kidneys, feet)

## When to seek urgent care

Very high or very low blood sugar with symptoms such as confusion, difficulty breathing,
persistent vomiting, or loss of consciousness is a medical emergency.

This document is a general educational summary and does not replace advice from a qualified
healthcare professional or an individualized treatment plan.
