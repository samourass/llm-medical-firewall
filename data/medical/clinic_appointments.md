# Clinic Appointments — General Information

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. General, fictional administrative information for this
> project's RAG demo only. Not derived from any real clinic, patient, or booking system.

This document describes general, administrative information about scheduling appointments at
a demonstration outpatient clinic.

## How appointments generally work

- Routine appointments (annual check-ups, follow-ups) are usually booked 1-3 weeks in advance.
- Urgent same-day slots are typically reserved for acute symptoms (fever, injury, sudden pain).
- Video/telehealth consultations are commonly available for follow-ups and medication reviews
  that do not require a physical exam.
- Patients are generally asked to arrive 10-15 minutes early for in-person visits to complete
  any required paperwork.

## What to bring to a visit (general guidance)

- A valid form of identification.
- An up-to-date list of current medications.
- Insurance information, if applicable in the relevant healthcare system.
- Any recent test results from other providers, if relevant.

## Cancellations and rescheduling

- Clinics commonly ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a routine
  appointment, to free the slot for another patient.
