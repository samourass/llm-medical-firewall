# Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) — General Information

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. General public-health information for this project's RAG
> demo only. Not medical advice, and not derived from any real patient record.

Hypertension means the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high. It is
often called a "silent" condition because it frequently causes no obvious symptoms.

## Typical blood pressure categories (general reference, not diagnostic)

- Normal: below 120/80 mmHg
- Elevated: 120-129 systolic and below 80 diastolic
- Stage 1 hypertension: 130-139 systolic or 80-89 diastolic
- Stage 2 hypertension: 140/90 mmHg or higher

These ranges are general educational reference values; only a clinician can interpret an
individual's readings.

## Lifestyle factors commonly discussed with patients

- Reducing sodium intake
- Regular physical activity
- Maintaining a healthy weight
- Limiting alcohol consumption
- Managing stress
- Not smoking

## When to seek urgent care

A very high reading (for example above 180/120 mmHg) accompanied by symptoms such as severe
headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, or vision changes is a medical emergency and
requires immediate care.

This document is a general educational summary and does not replace advice from a qualified
healthcare professional or personalized treatment plans.
