# Seasonal Influenza (Flu) — General Information

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. General public-health information for this project's RAG
> demo only. Not medical advice, and not derived from any real patient record.

Seasonal influenza is a contagious respiratory illness caused by influenza viruses. It spreads
mainly through droplets produced when infected people cough, sneeze, or talk.

## Common symptoms

- Fever or feeling feverish, chills
- Cough, sore throat
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Muscle or body aches
- Headaches
- Fatigue

Symptoms usually appear one to four days after exposure and most people recover within about
a week, although cough and fatigue can last longer.

## General self-care guidance

- Rest and stay hydrated.
- Over-the-counter fever reducers (such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen) can help with fever and
  aches; always follow the dosing instructions on the packaging or a pharmacist's advice.
- Stay home while symptomatic to avoid spreading the virus to others.
- Seek prompt medical care for difficulty breathing, persistent chest pain, confusion, or
  symptoms that improve then suddenly worsen.

## Prevention

- Annual flu vaccination is the most effective way to reduce the risk of infection and
  complications.
- Frequent handwashing and covering coughs/sneezes reduce transmission.

This document is a general educational summary and does not replace advice from a qualified
healthcare professional.
